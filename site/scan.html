Scan your face

Before you start. Your camera feed is analysed on this device by models running in your browser. No photo or video is uploaded or saved. The score is an estimate from a model trained on other people's ratings, not a measure of your worth.
  1. Face a window or a lamp so your face is evenly lit.
  2. Hold the camera at eye level, about an arm's length away.
  3. Remove glasses if you can, keep hair off your face, and look straight at the lens.

By continuing you confirm you are 16 or older and scanning your own face.

Line up your face

The scan starts by itself once every check is green and you hold still for a moment.

  • One face in view
  • Close enough (face fills the oval)
  • Centred in the frame
  • Looking straight at the camera
  • Enough light on your face
  • Holding still
