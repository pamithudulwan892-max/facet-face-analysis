Face analysis that stays on your device

An honest read of your face, and what you can actually improve.

Facet scans your face through your camera, estimates how a panel of raters would score the photo, and breaks it down into symmetry, balance, skin, eye area and photo conditions, with practical tips for the parts you can change.

How it works

1

Scan

Your camera finds 478 points on your face. Guides help you get good light and a straight angle, then it averages 8 frames for a steady reading.

2

Measure

It measures symmetry, proportions, skin texture and evenness, the under-eye area and photo quality, and compares them with 5,500 faces from a published research dataset.

3

Improve

You get a score with an honest margin of error, a breakdown that separates what you can change from what you cannot, and tips ordered by impact.

Private by design

All analysis runs inside your browser using on-device models. Your camera feed and photos never leave your device. There are no accounts and no tracking. Results are kept only in the current tab and are gone when you close it.

Read the privacy details

Honest about its limits

Attractiveness ratings are subjective. The model learned from a panel of 60 raters scoring photos of Asian and Caucasian adults, so it carries their tastes and biases. Treat the score as a curiosity, and focus on the tips.

Accuracy and limitations

Ready when you are

Good light and a straight angle give the most accurate result.
